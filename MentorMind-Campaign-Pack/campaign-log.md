## Honest quality notes (my own audit, for the decision on what to remake)

- The biggest gap is the voice. It is flat TTS and does not feel emotion. A real human voice would lift Days 1 to 4 most.
- Length varies from 9 s to 33 s. Several days are long for social. Plan shorter cut-downs.
- Hooks: some days start slowly. First two seconds should hold the eye.
- Sound design is inconsistent (Day 7 paper rustle, Day 9 press thumps, others none).
- No designed cover frames yet. No captions burned in for sound-off viewing on the pure-picture days.
- There is no single flagship film (45 to 90 s) that shows the whole story for users and investors.
